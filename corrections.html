Corrections Policy

At DirectlyChat, we are committed to accuracy, transparency, and maintaining the trust of our readers. This Corrections Policy outlines how we handle errors in our content.

Our Commitment to Accuracy

We strive to ensure all information published on DirectlyChat is accurate, up-to-date, and reliable. Our content is thoroughly researched and reviewed before publication.

How We Handle Corrections

If an error is identified in any of our articles, guides, or tool descriptions, we will:

  • Promptly correct the error
  • Add a correction notice at the bottom of the article (if significant)
  • Update the "last modified" date

Minor vs. Major Corrections

Minor corrections (typos, grammar, formatting) are made without notice. Major corrections (factual errors, misleading statements) will include a visible correction note within the article explaining what was changed and why.

Reporting an Error

If you believe you have found an error in any of our content, please contact us at: directlychatsupport@gmail.com with the subject line "Correction". Please include the article URL and the specific error you identified.

Updating Third-Party Information

When we reference third-party information (WhatsApp features, Google policies, etc.), we rely on official sources. If these external sources change, we will update our content accordingly.

Review Cycle

All our blog content is reviewed quarterly for accuracy. Timely topics (like new WhatsApp features) are reviewed immediately after official announcements.
