FAQ's

Can I send a WhatsApp message without saving the number?

Yes. DirectlyChat opens a WhatsApp chat instantly without adding the number to your phone’s contact list. Just select the country, type the number, and tap send.

Is DirectlyChat free?

Absolutely. The tool is 100% free. There are no subscriptions, hidden fees, or usage limits.

Do you store my phone number or messages?

No. DirectlyChat does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Everything stays on your device.

Does it work with WhatsApp Business?

Yes. Use the “WA BUSINESS” button to open chats directly in WhatsApp Business.

If only one WhatsApp app is installed, both buttons will open that app.

Can I message international numbers?

Yes. DirectlyChat supports over 195 countries. Just pick the correct country code from the dropdown.

Do I need to create an account?

No registration, login, or personal information is required. You can use it immediately.

Is DirectlyChat safe?

Yes. The tool simply redirects you to WhatsApp’s official wa.me link. Your messages are only handled by WhatsApp, not by DirectlyChat.

Can I use it on Android, iPhone, and desktop?

Yes. It works on any device that supports WhatsApp – Android, iOS (iPhone/iPad), Windows, Mac, and Chromebooks.

Why use this instead of saving contacts?

Many conversations are temporary (e.g., delivery person, customer support, one‑time enquiry). DirectlyChat helps keep your contact list clean while allowing instant communication.

Does it work with WhatsApp Web?

Yes. On desktop browsers, clicking send will open WhatsApp Web (or the desktop app) without saving the number.

How It Works

Step 1 – Select the Country Code

Choose the country of the phone number from the dropdown. DirectlyChat includes almost every country in the world.

Step 2 – Enter the Mobile Number

Type the recipient’s phone number. Spaces, dashes, or brackets are automatically removed – no need to format perfectly.

Step 3 – Add an Optional Message

You can write a message before opening WhatsApp. It will be pre‑filled in the chat window, saving you time.

Step 4 – Choose WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business

Tap “SEND MESSAGE” to open regular WhatsApp or “WA BUSINESS” to open WhatsApp Business.

Step 5 – Start the Conversation

DirectlyChat redirects you directly to the chat screen. Type and send your message – no contact saving required.
