WhatsApp Privacy Guide 2026: 7 Settings You Must Change Right Now

✍️ Author: DirectlyChat

WhatsApp is used by over 2 billion people worldwide, making it a prime target for cybercriminals, phishing attacks, and privacy breaches. In January 2026, WhatsApp introduced Strict Account Settings – a powerful new lockdown mode that combines multiple security features into one toggle.

Whether you're a journalist, public figure, or just someone who values their privacy, this guide will walk you through 7 essential privacy settings you should change right now to protect your WhatsApp account.

⚠️ Important Note: Strict Account Settings is designed for users at higher risk of cyber attacks. WhatsApp states that "most people are not targeted by such attacks". However, these settings are available to everyone who wants maximum privacy.

1. Enable Strict Account Settings (New Lockdown Mode)

Released in January 2026, Strict Account Settings is WhatsApp's most powerful privacy feature yet. When enabled, it automatically applies the highest possible restrictions to your account.

🔧 How to enable Strict Account Settings:

  1. Open WhatsApp and go to Settings (gear icon)
  2. Tap on Privacy
  3. Scroll down and tap Advanced
  4. Toggle on Strict Account Settings

Note: Once enabled, individual features cannot be turned off separately. You can only change this setting from your primary device, not from WhatsApp Web or desktop.

What happens when you enable Strict Account Settings?

  • ✅ Last seen and online status – Automatically restricted to only your contacts
  • ✅ Profile photo and About – Hidden from non-contacts
  • ✅ Media from unknown senders – Blocked automatically (images, videos, documents)
  • ✅ Calls from unknown numbers – Silenced
  • ✅ Link previews – Disabled for security
  • ✅ High-volume unknown messages – Blocked
  • ✅ Group invites – Only contacts (or pre-selected contacts) can add you to groups
  • ✅ Two-step verification – Automatically enabled
  • ✅ Security code change notifications – Turned on
💡 Pro Tip: Strict Account Settings is ideal for journalists, activists, public figures, or anyone who has received suspicious messages or calls. Even if you're not high-risk, enabling it adds significant protection against phishing and malware attacks.

2. Enable Two-Step Verification

Two-step verification adds an extra layer of security to your WhatsApp account. Even if someone gets your SIM card or verification code, they can't access your account without your PIN.

🔧 How to enable Two-Step Verification:

  1. Go to Settings → Account → Two-step verification
  2. Tap Enable
  3. Create a 6-digit PIN (remember it!)
  4. Add an email address for recovery (optional but recommended)
💡 Pro Tip: Strict Account Settings automatically enables two-step verification. If you enabled Strict Mode, you already have this on.

3. Hide Your Last Seen & Online Status

Your "last seen" and "online" status tells everyone when you were last active on WhatsApp. If you value your privacy, you can hide this information.

🔧 How to hide Last Seen:

  1. Go to Settings → Privacy → Last Seen & Online
  2. Select Nobody or My Contacts

Note: If you hide your last seen, you won't be able to see others' last seen either – it's a mutual setting.

4. Lock Specific Chats with Chat Lock

Chat Lock lets you move sensitive conversations to a separate folder that can only be accessed with your phone's fingerprint, face ID, or password.

🔧 How to lock a chat:

  1. Open the chat you want to lock
  2. Tap the contact/group name at the top
  3. Scroll down and tap Chat Lock
  4. Toggle on Lock this chat
  5. Authenticate with fingerprint/Face ID

Locked chats appear in a separate "Locked Chats" folder that you can only access with biometric authentication.

💡 Pro Tip: You can also enable Secret Code for Chat Lock – a custom password that hides the locked chats folder from your main chat list entirely.

5. Control Who Can Add You to Groups

Nobody likes being added to random spam groups. WhatsApp lets you control who can add you to group chats.

🔧 How to control group invites:

  1. Go to Settings → Privacy → Groups
  2. Select Nobody or My Contacts

If someone who isn't allowed tries to add you, they'll have to send you a private invite link instead.

6. Disable Read Receipts (Blue Ticks)

Blue ticks tell others when you've read their message. If you want to read messages without the sender knowing, you can disable read receipts.

🔧 How to disable read receipts:

  1. Go to Settings → Privacy
  2. Toggle off Read Receipts

Note: This also means you won't see read receipts from others. Group chats always show read receipts – this setting only applies to individual chats.

7. Hide Your Profile Photo & About

Your profile photo and "About" section are visible to everyone by default. You can restrict who sees this information.

🔧 How to hide profile photo & About:

  1. Go to Settings → Privacy
  2. Under Profile Photo, select Nobody or My Contacts
  3. Under About, select Nobody or My Contacts

Final Thoughts

WhatsApp's privacy features have evolved significantly in 2026. The new Strict Account Settings provides a one-tap lockdown option for users who need maximum protection. Even if you don't enable Strict Mode, the other six settings will dramatically improve your privacy.

Remember: privacy is personal. Choose the settings that match your comfort level. Most users will benefit from enabling two-step verification, hiding last seen, and controlling group invites. Journalists, activists, and public figures should consider Strict Account Settings for the highest level of protection.
